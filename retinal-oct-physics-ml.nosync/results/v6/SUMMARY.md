# v6 summary: image-level ROM and the hybrid model

Five findings (3b is the ablation behind finding 3). Every number is read from the JSON file named after it (all in `results/v6/`).

1. **Eigen-retinas carry less signal than the physics features.** On OCTID CSR/DR/MH, POD coefficients of the flattened image alone reach 0.696 balanced accuracy vs 0.872 for v5; adding them to v5 changes it by +0.015 [-0.010, +0.041], p = 0.141 (`image_pod_octid_dis750.json`). No other task gains (`image_pod_<task>.json`).
2. **A healthy-only POD flags disease without labels.** The residual e(x) (r = 25) separates NORMAL from ABNORMAL with AUROC 0.700 on Kermany, 0.696 on OCTDL and 0.691 from Kermany-healthy to OCTDL (`residual_auroc.json`), and its residual maps localise the departure. As a feature it adds +0.011 [+0.000, +0.027], p = 0.049 on OCTID (`residual_clf.json`).
3. **The headline ResNet-18 hybrid narrows but does not close the gap to the CNN.** On OCTID CSR/DR/MH it reaches 0.904 [0.865, 0.932] vs 0.935 [0.905, 0.961] for the fine-tuned ResNet-18 on the same folds; paired difference -0.036 [-0.076, +0.003], p = 0.966; on Kermany 4-class -0.141 [-0.162, -0.120], p = 1.000 and on the new scanner -0.172 [-0.206, -0.138], p = 1.000 (`ablation.json`). Against v5 it is significantly better on OCTID, OCTDL 7-class, Kermany 4-class, Kermany 3-disease, the new scanner (CI excludes zero), and the CI includes zero on OCTDL 6-disease; OCTID: +0.025 [+0.001, +0.050], p = 0.020. The DINOv2-S hybrid, pre-registered before the results, is reported in every table; ResNet-18 became the headline afterwards.
3b. **Ablation: physics adds accuracy on OCTDL and Kermany, not on OCTID.** Same frozen ResNet-18 with and without the physics + ROM features: significant gain on OCTDL 7-class, Kermany 4-class, Kermany 3-disease; CI includes zero on OCTID, OCTDL 6-disease, the new scanner (OCTID -0.018 [-0.062, +0.024], p = 0.779, `ablation.json`). Permutation importance (OCTID only) ranks "deep: ResNet-18 embedding (PCA 64)" first, but importance shows reliance, not added accuracy (`hybrid_importance.json`).
4. **The CNN does not look where the physics looks.** Grad-CAM mass by anatomical band vs the physics model's per-band importance: Spearman ρ = -0.22 (p = 0.48) over 12 band × class cells (`gradcam_octid_dis750.json`); the CNN puts most attention in the choroid band.
5. **A new scanner is still the hard case.** Kermany → OCTDL best configuration: v6 hybrid (ResNet-18 + physics) | none | no TTA = 0.753, DME recall 0.62 (`cross.json`), far below the fine-tuned CNN's 0.925 (`cnn_kermany_to_octdl.json`). Healthy-reference calibration raises DME recall but costs AMD recall and lowers balanced accuracy. Conformal triage on the ResNet-18 hybrid gives ABNORMAL coverage 0.945 for a 0.95 target on OCTDL, just under target (`conformal.json`).
